All good blogs deserve a webring page and here's mine.

- [log.fisher.sh](https://log.fisher.sh)
  - A Smart Dude with good opinions. Read his [post on why blogs are important](https://log.fisher.sh/posts/2026/06/12/you-need-a-corner-of-the-internet/) for a good place to start.
- [justinjackson.ca](https://justinjackson.ca/)
  - Of the posts I've read several have resonated with me. One of my favorites is ["I'm a fucking webmaster"](https://justinjackson.ca/webmaster/) which appeals to my nostalgic view of the scrappy early internet. 
- [solar.lowtechmagazine.com](https://solar.lowtechmagazine.com/)
  - A solar powered website that "... underscores the potential of past and often forgotten technologies and how they can inform sustainable energy practices." So far I have enjoyed the articles I've read and it vibes with my solar-punk proclivities.
